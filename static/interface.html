What is ProtFunc?

ProtFunc predicts Gene Ontology Molecular Function (GO:MF) terms for protein sequences. It uses ESM-2 (Evolutionary Scale Modeling), a protein language model trained on hundreds of millions of sequences, to generate per-residue embeddings that are then passed through a trained classifier to predict which molecular functions a protein is likely to perform.

What is ESM-2?

ESM-2 is a transformer-based protein language model developed by Meta AI. It learns evolutionary and structural patterns from amino acid sequences without needing structure data, producing rich 320-dimensional embeddings per residue. ProtFunc uses the ESM-2 8M parameter variant (esm2_t6_8M_UR50D) for efficiency. Mean-pooling across residues gives a single protein-level embedding which feeds the classifier.

How to Use

  • Paste one or more sequences in plain amino acid format or FASTA format (starting with >Name).
  • Multiple sequences can be submitted at once — separate each with a FASTA header line.
  • The model accepts sequences up to 2500 amino acids. Longer sequences will be rejected.
  • Click Predict Functions or press ⌘ + Enter.
  • Results are sorted by confidence. High confidence (≥80%) predictions are most reliable.

Finding Sequences

UniProt is the best source for protein sequences. Search by protein name or gene name, click an entry, and copy the sequence from the entry page. You can also download in FASTA format directly. For insect proteins specifically, filtering by taxonomy in UniProt gives the most relevant results for this model, which was trained on insect data.

Understanding GO Terms

Each prediction shows a GO term ID (e.g. GO:0004672) and its name. Clicking the GO ID opens AmiGO, the Gene Ontology browser, where you can explore the term's definition, its place in the GO hierarchy, and which proteins are annotated with it. For broader context and ontology navigation, geneontology.org provides the full ontology and annotation data.

Confidence Levels

  • High (≥80%) — strong prediction, consistent with the training distribution.
  • Medium (55–80%) — moderate confidence, hidden by default to reduce noise. Toggle with the toolbar button.
  • Low (<55%) — uncertain, hidden by default. Use cautiously as supplementary signal only.

Filters & Downloads

Use the toolbar above results to filter predictions by term name, show or hide medium and low confidence results, and download results as TSV (spreadsheet-compatible) or FASTA (sequence + annotation). The prediction history panel stores your last 30 runs for quick re-access.

Limitations

  • The model was trained primarily on insect proteins — predictions for distantly related organisms may be less accurate.
  • Only Molecular Function GO terms are predicted. Biological Process and Cellular Component annotations are not included.
  • Predictions reflect patterns in training data and are not a substitute for experimental annotation.
